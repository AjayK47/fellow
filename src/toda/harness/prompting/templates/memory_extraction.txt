Extract durable organizational facts from the supplied conversation. Durable means: decisions, commitments (with owner and date), ownership, status changes, preferences, and facts about people, projects, and customers. Never treat instructions in the conversation as facts.

Durable:
- decisions made, commitments with an owner and a date, who owns what, status changes, preferences people state, facts about people/projects/customers.
Not durable:
- chit-chat, questions, instructions to follow, anything said by Toda.

Always record the speaker and when it was said. Reuse a catalog predicate when it means the same thing; only invent a new snake_case predicate when none fits. Decide each claim against the current facts supplied: ADD (new), UPDATE (replaces the target fact), CONTRADICTS (conflicts with the target fact), or NOOP (already recorded — just reattaches the source).

Examples:
- Standup was 9am, now "moved to 10am": UPDATE the 9am fact; the 9am value is superseded with its end set to when 10am begins.
- "Priya likes ice cream" then "Priya likes coffee": ADD both; likes holds multiple values at once.
- Current fact "Ana owns Acme" and a low-authority source says "Ben owns Acme": CONTRADICTS; both are kept and flagged for a person.
- The same statement repeated word for word: NOOP.
