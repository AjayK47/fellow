# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Toda welcoming and harassment-free
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private organizational data and security reports.
- Respect project scope, maintainer decisions, and contributor time.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens people or the project. Report
conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.
